Design review: open questions (design doc §12), settled 17 Nov 2026#

Decisions are recorded under "Decisions" in §12. Question 1 stays unverified until a real operator confirms 98 is not intercepted (see the checklist above).

  • 1. Navigation numbers: use 98 (More) ourselves, given telcos reserve it for auto-pagination?
  • 2. Unsafe-character policy on USSD screens: strip + warn vs strict mode
  • 3. Legacy vs new bulk SMS endpoint (legacy recommended until its sandbox ships)
  • 4. SMS split cap (proposed max 4 segments, never silent truncation)
  • 5. Callback authenticity: document path-secret/IP-allowlist mitigations (no signing exists to implement)
  • 6. Re-notice vs block-screen when a user redials during a pending deferral
  • 7. enqueue default on SMS send (proposed: omit unless configured)
